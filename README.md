# 瘋人難防（《點石齋畫報》御七第50圖，1895 年，巴伐利亞邦立圖書館） / Feng ren nan fang [An Incident at a Paris Asylum]: Transcription Dataset

《點石齋畫報》御七〈瘋人難防〉描寫巴黎一所未具名的精神收治機構。記事交代就醫人數的報刊估計、分房關禁方式、病人離開房間後的群體事件，以及後續的院方與地方官回應。機構名稱及事件未由法文史料獨立核實。館方已有影像及公開機器 OCR。此次按圖重讀補足 OCR 的篇章定位與讀序，並分記原欄、圖字、印款與疑讀。

The Dianshizhai Pictorial describes an unnamed asylum in Paris. It reports an estimate of people seeking care, the use of separate rooms, the movement of residents out of those rooms during an incident, and the institutional and official response. The facility and incident have not been independently identified in French sources. The holding library already supplies its scan and machine OCR. This edition records complete article boundaries and source reading order, plus the column divisions, labels, marks, and uncertain readings.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。原紀年依原刊印「御七」。日本 CiNii 所列原御集範圍為 1895.1–1896.1；臺北市立美術館研究明指御集七約當 1895 年。出版日未定。　圖說正文、圖字、印與版記分列。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Text, caption, illustration labels, artist marks, seals, and page designations are described separately. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
